# Fee caps

`FEATURE_FEE_CAPS` defaults off. A daily fee above a verified cap shows a warning. Practice mode only logs it. Publishing and invoicing are not blocked. A monthly amount is not turned into a guessed daily fee.

Manitoba's $10 fee for 4 to 10 hours is from the December 8, 2024 maximum daily fee schedule. Ontario's $22 full-day CWELCC cap is from the 2026 Peel guideline citing O. Reg. 488/24. British Columbia has no single ceiling. Alberta's $15 figure is a placeholder.

The government page lists centres in `gov_province` that saved a fee above a verified cap. No child names.

Pages: `/centre-fee-caps`, `/gov/fee-caps`, `/admin-gov-fee-caps`.
