# Staff screening

`FEATURE_STAFF_SCREENING` defaults off. Practice mode warns and still allows a shift. `FEATURE_STAFF_SCREENING_LIVE=1` blocks a new shift when a saved record is expired or missing.

Texts need CASL SMS consent. Alberta's three-year criminal record and vulnerable sector check is cited from Alta. Reg. 143/2008. Other intervals are placeholders. The centre types the date on the document.

The government page shows expired and due-soon counts for `gov_province` only. It does not list staff names.

Pages: `/centre-screening`, `/gov/screening`, `/admin-gov-screening`.
