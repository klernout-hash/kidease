# Early learning frameworks

Fetched 1 November 2026 from the government pages linked below. KidEase stores short goal labels written in our own words, plus the link and the credit line. Full framework text is not stored. Licensed tools such as ASQ are not included.

A rule is **CONFIRMED** when the sentence below was read on the cited page. **PLACEHOLDER** means this pass did not confirm it.

## Manitoba

- Framework: Early Returns: Manitoba's Early Learning and Child Care Curriculum Framework for Preschool Centres and Nursery Schools. An infant booklet is listed on the same publications page.
- Year: **PLACEHOLDER**. The PDF pages opened did not print a year.
- Publisher: Manitoba Education and Early Childhood Learning.
- Framework URL: https://www.gov.mb.ca/education/childcare/resources/pubs/early_returns.pdf
- Publications list: https://www.manitoba.ca/education/childcare/resources/publications.html
- Curriculum statement for ages 2 to 6 (applications on or after 31 December 2011) and for infants (on or after 1 July 2013): **CONFIRMED**. Man. Reg. 62/86, s. 5(j) and s. 5(k).
- Daily program must match that statement: **CONFIRMED**. Man. Reg. 62/86, s. 10(2).
- Regulation: https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/_pdf-regs.php?reg=62/86
- Yearly review of the curriculum statement itself: **PLACEHOLDER**. The regulation does require a child's individual program plan to be reviewed at least yearly. That is a different document. KidEase logs a yearly reminder and does not file it.
- Whether the director accepts only Early Returns wording: **PLACEHOLDER**. The regulation says the statement must be acceptable to the provincial director.
