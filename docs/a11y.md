# Accessibility and languages

`FEATURE_A11Y_PASS` defaults **off**.

When it is on:

- A skip link points at `#main-content`. Labels exist in English, French, Simplified Chinese, Punjabi, and Tagalog.
- The help panel is a dialog with a name.
- Changing language announces the new language to a screen reader.

The skip target is on the page either way, so turning the flag on does not move layout.

## Audit

Checked 31 Oct 2026:

- Text `#1c2438` on `#f6f3ee`, muted `#5c6578` on `#fffcf8`, and white on `#1a3790` meet 4.5:1.
- English, French, and the eight extra packs already cover every copy key, including Simplified Chinese (`zh`), Punjabi (`pa`), and Tagalog (`tl`).
- The help launcher already has an accessible name. The close icon is hidden from the screen reader because the button has a label.
- Pinch zoom stays on. Touch targets on the new skip link are at least 44px when focused.
