# Canada child-care rules matrix

Access date for pages opened in this pass: **1 November 2026**.

A row is **CONFIRMED** only when the value was read on the cited page in this pass, or was already stored with a section citation that this pass did not contradict. **PLACEHOLDER** means the text was not opened, or the page does not state the number. KidEase does not invent a citation.

KidEase is an independent company. It is not a government, and no government approves or certifies KidEase. These notes are for review. They do not turn on any government tool.

The same 13 rules are listed for every province and territory.

## What changed in the app

- Ratio rows for British Columbia, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, and Yukon now cite the text above. Quebec, Newfoundland and Labrador, the Northwest Territories, and Nunavut stay placeholders.
- British Columbia's old straight 1:8 up to 25 children was wrong. Schedule E needs 3 staff at 17 to 25 children, not 4, and a different count below that.
- Manitoba's old single "toddler 1:6" row was only the separate-age 2-to-3 band. Mixed-age care is 1:4, 1:8, and 1:15.
- Nova Scotia infant maximum group size is 10, not 8. Serious incidents go to the Department within 24 hours.
- Prince Edward Island indoor ratios are confirmed. Maximum group size is not enforced because it was not in the section read.
- Fee figures that are real only for funded, designated, or subsidized programs are stored with a citation and are not treated as a cap on every centre.
- A listing no longer shows an unsourced monthly estimate, and it no longer says a licence is "official" or "active" without a check date.
- The footer states that KidEase is independent and not affiliated with any government, in English, French, and the eight other language packs.
- The home page no longer says "37,000+ centres listed". That count had no source, and the other languages said 20,000. The line now says the list comes from public registries.
