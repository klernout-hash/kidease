# Ratio monitor

`FEATURE_RATIO_MONITOR` defaults off. A director enters a child count and a staff count. KidEase does not read child names. Practice mode logs the director alert. `FEATURE_RATIO_MONITOR_LIVE=1` can text the director only when CASL SMS consent is on file.

Ontario infant rooms use 3 staff for up to 10 children (O. Reg. 137/15 Schedule 1). Alberta infants under 12 months are 1:3 with a maximum of 6 (Alta. Reg. 143/2008, Schedule 1, s. 27). Other provinces are placeholders.

The government monthly report is a count of breaches for `gov_province` only.

Pages: `/centre-ratios`, `/gov/ratios`, `/admin-gov-ratios`.
