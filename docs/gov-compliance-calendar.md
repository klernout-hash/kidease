# Compliance calendar

`FEATURE_COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR` defaults off. Practice mode logs 60, 30, and 7 day reminders. `FEATURE_COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR_LIVE=1` emails the daycare's listing address. No texts.

Manitoba licences last one year (M.R. 17/99, s. 4(7)). Ontario's maximum term is two years (O. Reg. 137/15, s. 85.2). Other terms are placeholders.

The government page shows overdue counts for `gov_province` only. There is no child information on these rows.

Pages: `/centre-compliance`, `/gov/compliance`, `/admin-gov-compliance`.
