# Confidentiality incident register

This is the KidEase register for confidentiality incidents under Quebec's Law 25 and for PIPEDA breach records. It is a template. It does not list a real incident.

The privacy officer is the person named on the privacy policy. Contact: privacy@kidease.ca.

Add a row only after a real incident is confirmed. Do not put names of children, health details, or message text in this file. Keep those in the private case notes.

## What each column means

- **Date found.** The date KidEase first knew about the incident.
- **Nature.** What happened, in plain words. Example categories: wrong recipient, lost device, unauthorized access, ransomware, public listing of a home address. Do not invent a category for a row that has no incident.
- **Personal information involved.** The kinds of data, not a dump of the data. Examples: parent email, child name, invoice amount.
- **People affected.** A count, or a description of the group, when that count is known. If the count is not known, write "not known yet". Do not guess a number.
- **Risk of serious injury.** The privacy officer's assessment: yes, no, or not decided. "Serious injury" includes humiliation, identity theft, or harm to a child. Say why in the private case notes, not in this public template.
- **CAI notice.** Whether the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec was notified, the date, or "not required" with the reason.
- **OPC notice.** Whether the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada was notified, the date, or "not required" with the reason.
- **Measures.** What was done to reduce the harm and to stop a repeat. No secrets, tokens, or database URLs.

## Empty on purpose

No incident is recorded here. A blank table is the current register, not a claim that an incident never happened outside this file. If an incident is handled only in email, copy the fields above into this register when the privacy officer confirms it.
