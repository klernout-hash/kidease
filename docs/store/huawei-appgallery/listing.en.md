## Full introduction

KidEase helps parents in Canada find licensed daycare. Search by city or by where you are. Save up to five centres. Message a daycare. Ask for a tour.

Parents can search, save, and message at no charge. Daycares can claim their listing and use the daycare tools during the free founding period. No card is charged. Paid extras are not sold in this app.

Fees, open spots, hours, and languages show only when that centre entered them. A $10-a-day label shows only when the listing data says that centre is in the program.

This app is for adults. Parents and daycare owners. Accounts are 18 or older. It is not a kids app.

Nothing is sold inside the app. There is no Huawei In-App Purchase in this version. Continue with Google is not in this Huawei app. Sign in with email. Huawei ID sign-in is not on.

The app opens https://www.kidease.ca. Message alerts and phone call rings are not sent in this version. SMS and in-app chat are coming soon.
