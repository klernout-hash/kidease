# Space demand

`FEATURE_DEMAND_REPORTS` defaults off. The quarterly report counts waiting families by area and age. A group under 5 is hidden, and a neighbouring group is hidden when one small cell could be worked out from the total. No names.

Practice mode logs the quarter. `FEATURE_DEMAND_REPORTS_LIVE=1` marks the run as ready to send. A government user sees only `gov_province`.

Pages: `/gov/demand`, `/admin-gov-demand`.
